What This Flow Does

This flow is the platform's security camera and record keeper. Every time a user or an admin does something important, like logging in, updating a profile, or submitting an application, the system writes it down in an audit log. The log records who did the action, what they did, and exactly when they did it.

For regular users, the log keeps track of their activity on the platform. For admins, it captures every management action they take, such as approving an application or updating an opportunity. These logs are stored in a dedicated database and are kept safe from tampering.

A background scheduler also runs periodically to clean up old logs according to a retention policy. This keeps the audit database from growing too large. The scheduler can also generate compliance reports that summarize activity over time for regulators or internal review.

This flow makes the platform transparent and accountable. If any question ever arises about who did what and when, the audit log provides a clear, unchangeable record. It is a key part of the platform's security and compliance strategy.
